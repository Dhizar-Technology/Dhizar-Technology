## About

Dhizar Technology designs and builds gaming controllers — from everyday gamepads to next-generation input devices powered by BCI, AR/VR, and AI. We're an early-stage company, and this organization is where our build happens in the open.

<br>

## What We're Building

| Team | Focus |
|---|---|
| 🔧 **Hardware Development** | Controller design, electronics, and prototyping |
| 🧠 **AI Model Development** | Signal classification for our BCI controller, built on **EEGNet** |
| 💻 **Software Development** | Company website & our in-house visualization/testing tool |

Three teams, working in parallel, toward one product line.
